# The exact target span has an unavoidable localization kernel

Status: new deduction from the established results at PR head 1d7e07950f5b097fc83fa946946885a91a6d91fb; independently audited (see [audit](../verification/target_span_obstruction_review.md)). This is an obstruction to a proposed proof strategy, not a classification or a counterexample to full-depth-two equality.

## Hypotheses and inputs

Let a be an integer congruent to 3 modulo 8, different from 3. Suppose E_a: Y²=X³+(a−3)X²−(a−3)X is geometrically non-CM and has rational rank one. Let p be an odd good prime congruent to 1 modulo 4. Put C_a: y²=x⁶+a x⁴+a x²+1, K=Q(i), R=Sym²V_p(E_a), and select K→Q_p. Write W=(i,0) in that embedding. Use exactly the repository's Mordell–Weil abelian-image and actual-local-image convention.

The inputs are:

1. The isomorphism lambda_S: H¹_f,S(Q,R) → ⊕_(q∈S)H¹(Q_q,R), and the two-dimensional finite local space H¹_f(Q_p,R), from noncm_residual_gluing.md.
2. The compatible Tate lift from integral_tate_lift.md.
3. The exact odd-prime actual images from odd_bad_residual_images.md and the zero actual dyadic joint image from dyadic_actual_images.md. 
4. The global twisted discrepancy eta in H¹(Q,R⊗chi_-4), its anti-invariant restriction eta_K in H¹(K,R), and its selected p-localization delta_p(W), from twisted_discrepancy_descent.md. The class eta_K is nonzero if an odd prime divides a−3, since it is ramified at that prime after passage to K.

Choose S not containing p and containing all bad primes. Let Q be the set of odd primes q dividing a−3 with q congruent to 1 modulo 4, and let m=|Q|. Choose i_q in Q_q and e_q=r_q((i_q,0))≠0. Put n_q=v_q(a−3) and

    C_q={0,1,−1} ∪ {±4k/n_q: k is an integer and 1≤k<n_q/4}.

Signs of i_q do not matter since C_q is symmetric. Let beta_q=lambda_S^(-1)((0,...,e_q,...,0)), and let beta_q,K be its restriction to K.

## Proposition: finite affine-grid criterion

If m≥1, then:

(a) The m+1 classes eta_K and beta_q,K are linearly independent over Q_p.

(b) For every vector c=(c_q) in Q_p^m, the class

    xi_c=eta_K−Σ_(q∈Q)c_q beta_q,K

is globally nonzero. This remains true in particular for every actual target vector c∈∏C_q.

(c) Full-depth-two membership is exactly

    W∈C_a(Q_p)_2  iff  there is c∈∏C_q with loc_v(xi_c)=0.

(d) For L=span_Qp(eta_K,beta_q,K), localization has

    dim ker(loc_v:L→H¹_f(Q_p,R)) ≥ m−1.

Consequently, when m≥2, injectivity is impossible even on this specific space generated by the distinguished discrepancy and the actual target directions. Enlarging to a relaxed Selmer group is not the source of this obstruction.

## Proof

The classes beta_q are independent: applying lambda_S to a relation separates its summands, and each e_q is nonzero. Restriction H¹(Q,R)→H¹(K,R) is injective. Its kernel is H¹(Gal(K/Q),R^(G_K)), which vanishes by finite-group averaging over Q_p. Thus the beta_q,K remain independent.

Each beta_q,K belongs to the +1 eigenspace for Gal(K/Q), because it descends from Q. The twisted construction places eta_K in the −1 eigenspace. Since m≥1, there is an odd prime dividing a−3, and the established ramification theorem gives eta_K≠0. Applying the two idempotents (1±c)/2 proves (a) and (b). The action of c here is arithmetic conjugation on global cohomology, not an action on the chosen completion Q_p.

All actual residual images at 2 and at nonsplit odd primes are zero. The only remaining images are C_q e_q, with zero Tate coordinate at those primes. The compatible Tate-lift theorem and exact joint gluing therefore identify full membership with

    delta_p(W)∈{Σ c_q loc_p(beta_q): c_q∈C_q}.

Localization of eta_K at the selected v is delta_p(W); localization of beta_q,K is loc_p(beta_q), since K_v=Q_p. This proves (c), with no replacement of joint images by independently chosen projections.

The beta_q are finite at p by construction; eta_K is finite at v by the crystalline discrepancy result. Hence loc_v(L) lies in H¹_f(Q_p,R), which has dimension two. Rank-nullity and (a) prove (d).

## What this rules out, and what it does not

Proving that every xi_c is globally nonzero supplies no further exclusion: that nonvanishing is already automatic. A proof invoking localization injectivity on the whole span L is untenable as soon as there are two split target primes.

A nonzero kernel does NOT prove a mock point exists. It might lie wholly in span(beta_q,K), rather than intersect the affine hyperplane with eta_K coefficient one. Even if that affine hyperplane intersects the kernel, the intersection might miss the finite rational grid ∏C_q. The precise arithmetic question is this finite-grid intersection, not a comparison of dimensions.

Equivalently, write v_q=loc_p(beta_q) and d=delta_p(W). If d is not in span_Qp(v_q), exclusion follows immediately. If it is in that span, choose one solution c0 to Σc_qv_q=d. The allowable coefficient vectors are exactly

    (c0+ker[Q_p^m→H¹_f(Q_p,R)]) ∩ ∏C_q.

If the target-direction image has rank r, this affine solution set has dimension m−r when nonempty. In particular, rank two forces an affine solution over Q_p, but says nothing about intersection with the finite grid. This is finite and exact, but evaluating it requires the actual transported periods v_q. A nonzero coordinate of d alone does not determine it.

The conjugate completion cannot be silently imposed as an extra condition. Under the conjugate identifications, the two localizations of xi_c are (d−Σc_qv_q, −d−Σc_qv_q). A zero at the selected place need not be a zero at the other. Membership of W_− can use the opposite global target vector −c, rather than the same lift. Thus global arithmetic conjugation does not turn the selected-place criterion into simultaneous vanishing at both places.

Finally, since every C_q contains 0 and ±1, L is exactly the linear span of the finite collection {xi_c:c∈∏C_q}; it is not an artificially enlarged space. The affine span of that collection is eta_K+span(beta_q,K).

For m=1 and d≠0, nonzero wedge d∧v_q excludes W; wedge zero alone does not prove membership, because the scalar ratio must belong to C_q, and v_q itself could vanish. For m≥2, no determinant-only replacement decides the finite-grid condition.

## Consequence for the research program

The existing uniform period theorems at 5 and 13 evaluate a coordinate of d. They do not compute the transported vectors v_q. Additional local period examples of d cannot close the nonzero-target cases without either computing these transport periods or proving a theorem separating d from their finite rational combinations. This proposition identifies a precise obstruction to an appealing global-local shortcut, while leaving the original general classification open.
