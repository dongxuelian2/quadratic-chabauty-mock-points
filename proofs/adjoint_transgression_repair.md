# An explicit missing descent equation for the actual nonzero-target example

Status: elementary finite-group and Frobenius calculations independently checked. The final mod25 strengthening has a separate exact exhaustive check. No finite Selmer dimension or full-depth-two classification is asserted.

## 1. A concrete target, not a hypothetical Galois image

Take a=−157469 and E:Y²=X³+AX²−AX, with A=−157472=−32·7·19·37. The established odd/dyadic residual image theorems leave exactly the target grid {0,±e_37}; hence the remaining W=(i,0) comparison at5 is with {0,±Theta_5(e_37)}. The rank certificate is already in the repository. None of that rank input is needed for the finite-group computation below.

At the good primes3 and17, exact point counts give #E(F_3)=6 and #E(F_17)=16. Thus traces are−2 and2. The characteristic polynomials modulo5 have projective companion-matrix orders6 and4 respectively. Equivalently the trace-squared/determinant ratios are3 and2 in F_5.

The projective mod5 image H⊂PGL_2(F_5)≅S_5 is therefore the whole group. An intransitive subgroup of S_5 is contained in S_4 or S_3×S_2, neither of which contains both orders4 and6. A transitive subgroup containing both orders has order divisible by60, hence order60 or120. The unique index-two subgroup A_5 has no element of order6, so order120 is forced.

This particular curve is ordinary at5: #E(F_5)=8, with trace−2. The supersingular reduction in the separate a≡0 modulo5 family must not be substituted here.

## 2. Exact finite cohomology

Let G=PGL_2(F_5) and M=ad⁰(F_5²), with conjugation action. A Sylow subgroup P=C_5 is generated by u=[[1,1],[0,1]]. In the basis e=[[0,1],[0,0]], h=diag(1,−1), f=[[0,0],[1,0]], conjugation is

    U=[[1,3,4],[0,1,1],[0,0,1]].

It is a single Jordan block of size3. Consequently N=1+U+U²+U³+U⁴=0,

    H¹(P,M)=M/(u−1)M=F_5[f],
    H²(P,M)=M^P=F_5 e.

The normalizer is generated by P and d=diag(2,1). Since d u d^(-1)=u², the induced cohomology action uses u↦u³. On H¹ it has multiplier3·3=4. On H² it has multiplier3·2=1. The degree-two factor3 follows from the cyclic extension presentation: replacing the generator by its third power multiplies its fifth-power relation by3, modulo the norm.

Distinct Sylow5-subgroups intersect trivially. Thus the stable-elements theorem in positive degree reduces to invariance under this normalizer. It follows that

    H¹(G,M)=0,       H²(G,M)≅F_5.

The cohomology calculation is a proof, not a numerical rank estimate. The companion checker verifies the matrices, norm, ranks and normalizer conjugation exactly.

## 3. Repairing inflation–restriction with one explicit equation

Let L be the projective mod5 splitting field and N_Gal=G_L, so G_Q/N_Gal=G. Inflation–restriction is

    0 → H¹(G_Q,M) → Hom_G(N_Gal,M) → H²(G,M).

The last arrow cannot simply be omitted. Choose an element sigma∈G_Q mapping to u. For an equivariant continuous homomorphism F:N_Gal→M, restriction of its transgression to P is the cyclic extension class represented by

    ±F(sigma⁵) ∈ M^u/NM = F_5 e.

The harmless sign depends on the cocycle convention. Restriction from H²(G,M) to H²(P,M) is injective, since the Sylow index24 is prime to5. Therefore

    F comes from H¹(G_Q,M)  if and only if  F(sigma⁵)=0.

This is a single homogeneous F_5-linear equation on any computed equivariant ray-class space. Replacing sigma by n sigma changes the value by N F(n)=0, so the obstruction is independent of that lift. Its value is u-invariant by equivariance and sigma sigma⁵=sigma⁵ sigma.

Implementing the equation requires a compatible lift/extension calculation in the ray-class presentation. The formula does not supply that number-field computation by itself.

## 4. An explicit nonzero unrestricted transgression

The projective representation on E[25] takes values in PGL_2(Z/25Z). Its restriction to N_Gal lands in the kernel of reduction to PGL_2(F_5), canonically identified with M_2(F_5)/F_5·I≅M via the trace-zero projection. This gives an equivariant homomorphism F_0:N_Gal→M.

Every projective lift of u modulo25 has fifth power I+5e. Indeed the standard integral lift has that fifth power, and changing the lift by I+5B changes the result by5NB, which is zero projectively because the adjoint norm is zero. The companion checker also enumerates all125 trace-zero kernel representatives and verifies the identity directly.

Consequently F_0(sigma⁵)=e≠0. Thus the unrestricted transgression is actually surjective onto F_5; the one equation is not globally vacuous.

This does NOT assert that F_0 satisfies the required finite local conditions or belongs to any specific ray-class subspace. Such local conditions might already exclude it. No Selmer dimension is inferred from this unrestricted witness.

## 5. Consequence for available software and remaining calculation

Combes, *Computing Selmer groups associated to mod p Galois representations*, arXiv:2512.12899v2, Section4.1, simplifies inflation–restriction by assuming finite H¹ and H² vanish. The second assumption fails for this actual adjoint example. The published local systems also do not automatically identify the propagated integral Bloch–Kato conditions required here. Therefore running those routines without adapting both descent and local conditions would not certify the desired target comparison.

The next exact computation must construct the appropriate equivariant ray-class space, impose F(sigma⁵)=0, and impose all required propagated local conditions. Class-group calculations must be certified if an unconditional result is claimed. This note provides an explicit missing descent equation, not an implemented ray-class or crystalline-local backend.
