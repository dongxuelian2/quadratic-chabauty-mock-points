# Local lifting at 19 and a smaller adjoint ray modulus

Let a=-157469, A=a-3=-157472 and

    E: Y²=X³+AX²-AX,
    T=ad⁰(T₅E),  M_n=T/5ⁿT,  M=M_1.

Use continuous cohomology and arithmetic Frobenius. The lattice is the
trace-zero endomorphism lattice of the actual Tate module, not a trivial
lift of the residual representation. Keep S={2,5,7,19,37,39367,∞}.
Let F and L=F(sqrt(5)) be the fields in the
[degree-ten reduction](adjoint_quintic_reduction.md), with involution tau.

**Theorem.**

1. For every n>=1,

   | prime q | H¹(Q_q,M_n) |
   |---|---|
   | 7 | 0 |
   | 19 | Z/5Z |
   | 37 | Z/5ⁿZ, entirely unramified |
   | 39367 | 0 |

   Every coefficient-reduction map
   `H¹(Q₁₉,M_(n+1)) -> H¹(Q₁₉,M_n)` is zero. In particular
   `H¹(Q₁₉,T)=0`, and any class in `H¹(G_{Q,S},M)` lifting to M_2
   must restrict to zero at 19.

2. There is one prime P5 of L above 5, of ramification index 10.
   There are two primes Q19 and tau(Q19) above 19 with `(e,f)=(2,2)`.
   With

       m0 = P5^13 * Q19 * tau(Q19),

   the existing global reduction sharpens to

       H¹(G_{Q,S},M) ≅ Hom(Cl_m0(L)/5,F₅)^-.

   Under this same isomorphism,

       ker(loc_19) ≅ Hom(Cl_(P5^13)(L)/5,F₅)^-.       (1)

   Thus the image of `H¹(G_{Q,S},M_2) -> H¹(G_{Q,S},M)` is contained
   in the right side of (1), and so is the reduction of `H¹(G_{Q,S},T)`.

The prime-decomposition data in part 2 are exact software-assisted
arithmetic, certified below by ideal products and residue fields. Part 1
has a direct local-representation proof. No class group, ray-group
dimension, or finite/crystalline Selmer group is computed. The inclusion
for liftable classes need not be equality; the localization functional
at 19 might even vanish on the entire global space. Neither a strict
drop in global dimension nor the target comparison is asserted.

## 1. Quartic good reduction at 7, 19 and 37

At each of these primes `v_q(A)=1`. Over the tame extension containing
pi with pi⁴=A, substitute `X=pi² Z`, `Y=pi³ W`. The exact equation is

    W²=Z³+pi² Z²-Z.

Its special fibre `W²=Z³-Z` has discriminant 64, a unit at q. A tame
inertia generator induces the order-four automorphism
`(Z,W) -> (-Z,iW)` or its inverse. Its action J on T₅E satisfies
`J²=-1` and has characteristic polynomial `t²+1`. Since 5 is odd,
the inertia-fixed trace-zero endomorphisms are the free line Z₅ J.
This description commutes with reduction modulo every 5ⁿ: the inertia
image on T has order two, prime to 5, so averaging is exact.

Arithmetic Frobenius conjugates J to J^q. It therefore acts on this
line as epsilon_q, where epsilon_q=1 for q=1 mod4 and -1 for q=3 mod4.
Wild inertia acts trivially, since the displayed good-reduction
extension is tame.

Put R_n=Z/5ⁿZ. The tame-inertia and unramified cohomology sequences give

    0 -> R_n/(epsilon_q-1)R_n -> H¹(Q_q,M_n)
      -> ker(epsilon_q-q : R_n -> R_n) -> 0.          (2)

To see the twist in the right term, the pro-5 part of tame inertia is
Z₅(1). Its degree-one cohomology is the inertia-invariant line with
Frobenius action epsilon_q/q. The complementary prime-to-5 inertia
has no positive-degree cohomology. Apply the unramified quotient to
this calculation. This is the usual inertia inflation-restriction
sequence; see Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, I, proof of
Lemma 2.9, printed pages 31–32, for the exact sequence used here.

For q=7, epsilon=-1 and both -2 and -8 are 5-adic units, giving zero.
For q=37, epsilon=1 and 1-37=-36 is a unit, leaving R_n in the
unramified term. For q=19 the left term is zero and restriction to
inertia identifies the group naturally with

    ker(20:R_n->R_n) = 5^(n-1)R_n.                  (3)

It has order five. Reduction from R_(n+1) to R_n sends its subgroup
5ⁿR_(n+1) to zero. Naturality of restriction proves the stated zero
map on H¹. Passing to the inverse limit gives H¹(Q₁₉,T)=0;
the finite-level invariant groups here are zero, so no inverse-limit
correction occurs. Localization and coefficient reduction commute,
proving the necessary global lifting condition.

This also explains why merely counting residual local dimensions is
insufficient: at 19 a nonzero residual class exists at each coefficient
level, but none forms a nonzero compatible tower.

## 2. Multiplicative reduction at 39367

The discriminant and c4 are

    Delta=16 A³(A+4),  c4=16 A(A+3).

At q=39367 they have valuations 1 and 0. Thus the model is minimal
with multiplicative reduction and Tate-parameter valuation one.
The possible unramified quadratic twist disappears on the adjoint
representation. In a Tate basis, inertia acts on T₅E by
`u=[[1,1],[0,1]]` for a generator of its pro-5 tame quotient, and
Frobenius has diagonal entries q,1. Its off-diagonal entry can be
removed since q-1 is a 5-adic unit.

On trace-zero endomorphisms the inertia-fixed line is generated by
`e=[[0,1],[0,0]]`, with Frobenius eigenvalue q. The inertia coinvariant
line is generated by the class of `f=[[0,0],[1,0]]`, with eigenvalue
q^(-1). The image of u-1 has rank two and is saturated over Z₅:
in the basis e,h,f its nonzero elementary divisors are units. Thus
these descriptions remain valid over every R_n.

The unramified term of H¹ is killed by the unit q-1. In H¹ of inertia
the extra inverse cyclotomic factor makes the Frobenius eigenvalue
q^(-2). Since q=2 modulo5, q²-1 is also a unit, and the ramified term
vanishes. Hence H¹(Q_q,M_n)=0 for every n. The Tate uniformization
used here is recalled in Milne, *Elliptic Curves*, second edition,
Chapter III, pages 102–103.

## 3. Exact prime decomposition and the conductor reduction

The maximal order of L and the following decompositions are computed
with exact arithmetic by `scripts/check_adjoint_local_decomposition.jl`.
Repeated pairs denote distinct prime ideals.

| rational prime | pairs (ramification index, residue degree) in L |
|---|---|
| 2 | (1,2), (4,2) |
| 5 | (10,1) |
| 7 | (1,2), (2,2), (2,2) |
| 19 | (1,1), (1,1), (2,2), (2,2) |
| 37 | (1,2), (2,2), (2,2) |
| 39367 | (5,2) |

For each row the checker verifies `prod P^e=q O_L`, constructs the
residue fields, checks their orders against `Norm(P)=q^f`, and checks
that the sum of ef is ten. It also verifies that tau exchanges the
two `(2,2)` primes above 19. No class-number or GRH hypothesis enters.

Away from 5, principal units are pro-q and admit no nonzero continuous
homomorphism to the additive group F₅. A ramified F₅-valued character
therefore factors on inertia through the residue-field units; it can
be nonzero only when `Norm(P)=1 mod5`. Every norm above 2,7,37,39367
is 4 modulo5, so these places contribute no ramification at all. The
two `(1,1)` places above 19 also have norm 4 modulo5. Only Q19 and its
conjugate can contribute a tame conductor factor, each to exponent one.

At P5, normalize valuation by `v(P5)=1`, so `v(5)=e=10`. The local
logarithm and exponential identify `U^n=1+P5^n` with `P5^n` for
`n>e/(5-1)`. Taking n=3, fifth powering maps U³ onto U¹³. Every
F₅-valued character kills fifth powers and hence kills U¹³. There is
no odd-order ramification at an infinite place. Consequently m0
contains every conductor in the unrestricted character calculation.
Global class field theory and the previously proved equivariant
cohomological isomorphism give part 2's unrestricted assertion.

## 4. Why removing the 19 factors is exactly the local zero condition

At 19 the character chi of Q(sqrt(5)) is trivial. In the notation of
the degree-ten reduction, let W be the five-point permutation module
and B=W₀ tensor chi. The exact sequences are

    0 -> B -> W tensor chi -> chi -> 0,
    0 -> chi -> B -> M -> 0.

Locally at 19 the quintic algebra F has a degree-one factor and one
factor of ramification index two and residue degree two. Indeed 5 is
a square in Q₁₉, so L splits locally into two copies of F, and the
preceding exact decomposition gives these factors. Thus H⁰(W tensor
chi) -> H⁰(chi) is onto: its degree-one orbit already maps onto.

Here H⁰(M)=0 by the epsilon=-1 calculation, H¹(chi) is the
one-dimensional unramified space, and H²(chi)=0 because 19 is not 1
modulo5. The local long exact sequences consequently identify

    H¹(B) = ker(H¹(W tensor chi) -> H¹(chi)),
    H¹(M) = H¹(B) / H¹(chi).                         (4)

By local Shapiro, H¹(W tensor chi) has dimension three: the degree-one
factor contributes one unramified character; the degree-four factor
contributes one unramified and one tame character, because 19²=1 mod5.
Its unramified subspace has dimension two. The map to H¹(chi) is onto
even on this subspace, again using the degree-one factor. Its kernel
inside the unramified subspace is therefore a line.

The injected H¹(chi) in (4) is nonzero and unramified, so it is precisely
that line. Hence, for a class in the kernel in (4), its image in H¹(M)
is zero exactly when its Shapiro character is unramified. Restriction
to L preserves this property; the two quartic places are interchanged
by tau, and anti-invariance ties their tame characters together.
It follows that loc_19 is zero exactly when both Q19 factors can be
removed from the character's conductor. This proves (1).

This argument uses the actual local correction terms. In particular,
the global vanishing of H¹(chi) from the earlier theorem is not
incorrectly imposed on the local group at 19.

## 5. Reproduction and stopping boundary

Run the exact finite-module checks and the bounded field calculation:

    python scripts/check_adjoint_local_lifting.py
    julia --project=<Hecke environment> scripts/check_adjoint_local_decomposition.jl

The first independently enumerates the mod-five cocycles at 19 and
uses integer Smith forms at moduli 5,25,125,625 to verify all four
local group orders. It checks the natural zero reduction map, not
just equality of group sizes. The second computes no class or ray
group. The proof for every n is the argument above, not extrapolation
from these four finite levels.

The two long class-group attempts were stopped on 11 November 2026.
The original run had reached full relation rank but was spending over
an hour in the sparse exact-index calculation without further algorithm
output. The patched smaller-base run had not reached full rank. Its
last saved checkpoint contains 1,331 exact relations at rank 569;
the last ordinary progress output before interruption indicated rank
601 of 2,449. Logs and the checkpoint remain in ignored build/; the
original process had no recoverable relation checkpoint. Neither run
produced a class group or ray dimension.

The smaller modulus does not eliminate the global class-group problem.
It also does not impose the crystalline condition at 5, prove a residual
class lifts globally, or evaluate delta₅(W) against `{0,±Theta₅(e₃₇)}`.
Those are separate requirements for the original non-CM target. The
long computations are stopped rather than restarted on this modulus.
