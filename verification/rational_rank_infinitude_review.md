# Review of the rational-parameter infinitude transfer

The source was independently checked at Zywina, arXiv:2505.16960v1, Section 1.1, Theorem 1.5, Lemma 4.1 and the end of Section 4. The relevant proof actually constructs infinitely many specializations in the stated family with 0<t<1 and distinct j-invariants; it is not merely an existence result for unrelated elliptic curves.

The transfer identities, inverse Möbius parameter map, discriminants, both elliptic quotient maps and differential pullbacks were independently recomputed with exact symbolic arithmetic. The standalone checker passes. Isogeny invariance of rank and the independent differential pullbacks give Jacobian rank exactly two.

The nonconstant quotient j-function has finite fibers. Finiteness of the automorphism group of a fixed smooth genus-two curve justifies the final claim of infinitely many geometric curve isomorphism classes.

Scope checked: only rational parameters are proved infinite. The parameter interval contains finitely many integers. No arbitrary extra local approximation, integral prime-pattern infinitude, denominator-prime local target, or full depth-two endpoint equality is inferred. The external infinitude input is explicitly credited.
