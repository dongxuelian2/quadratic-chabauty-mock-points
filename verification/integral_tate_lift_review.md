# Independent audit: compatible Tate lift for the integral family

Verdict: approved for integral a=3 modulo8, geometrically non-CM rank-one E_a, and odd good p=1 modulo4, in the stated Mordell-Weil-restricted actual-local convention.

## Global lifting

Finite-level Kummer identifies H²(G_T,mu_(p^n)) with the p^n-torsion of Br(Z[1/T]) because Pic is zero. Brauer–Hasse–Noether gives localization injectivity. Finite H¹ groups satisfy Mittag–Leffler, so passage to Z_p(1), followed by Q_p, retains it. The actual point supplies a local lift at p, and zero abelian classes have neutral lifts elsewhere.

Kummer classes of p and2 span H¹(Q_p,Q_p(1)) by valuation and nonzero log_p(2). Central adjustment therefore produces a crystalline lift. The S-unit valuation isomorphism then uniquely kills all bad-local classes. H_f¹(Q,Q_p(1)^2)=0 proves uniqueness.

## Entire Tate fiber is detected

The involution matrices are diag(1,-1) and minus exchange. Their graph differences span the two trace-zero Rosati-symmetric directions, hence both central Tate functionals. At p their height translations detect the two-dimensional finite central fiber. Away from p the analogous maps use valuation times nonzero log_p(q).

## Arbitrary-torsor height comparison

Balakrishnan–Dogra I, Definition5.3 and Section5.2, define the path-module twist for arbitrary torsors and compute its adjacent classes from the abelian class and fixed base module. Section4.3.3 supplies both global factorization and central translation. These statements were checked directly in the primary source.

Both independent coordinates force d=0.

## Same-point compatibility

The odd q|a-3 theorem gives all joint pairs (0,r). At2 both scalar local functions vanish identically, so the independent central functionals give zero full Tate coordinate. At q|a+1 the residual coordinate is zero and the basepoint supplies (0,0). Good local classes are neutral. Thus the residual lift glues with t_zero while preserving actual same-point conditions.
