# Independent review: non-CM residual localization and gluing

The proof was independently reviewed after its derivation, with direct checks of Newton–Thorne arXiv:1912.11265v3 Theorem B(2), and Bellaïche Proposition 2.7 / Theorem 2.2. The corrected core passed review.

## Checked steps

- The proved adjoint Selmer vanishing, R^*(1)=ad^0 V, and the exact 2-2 dimension calculation give H_f^1(Q,R)=0 without an extra conjecture.
- The relaxed dual Selmer group is contained in the zero strict adjoint group. Injectivity and equal dimensions therefore prove the bad-prime localization isomorphism.
- At potentially multiplicative primes the Tate monodromy/Frobenius commutant is scalar, giving H^1(Q_q,R)=0.
- The rational involution fixes the basepoint and gives a canonical V1 section in U_R. The reference class has trivial localization away from p.
- H^0(G,H)=0 gives ordinary cohomological fiber-product gluing. It does NOT alone prove crystalline gluing.
- Exactness of D_cris for the crystalline Lie extension provides the invariant B_cris lift that aligns the two crystalline trivializations. This proves finite-condition gluing, and similarly justifies crystallinity of the residual central difference.
- The full criterion quantifies ONE global Tate class and imposes loc_q(t)=tau_q at EVERY selected joint local pair. It therefore retains the same-local-point condition.

## Correction incorporated before publication

Full H^1(Q_q,R) need not vanish at a good prime. Only its unramified finite subgroup, and consequently the good-prime actual curve image, vanish. The support reduction is explicitly a reduction of actual residual contributions. Artificially enlarging S by good primes does not justify declaring their full H^1 zero.

All Tate bad-prime conditions stay in S even when the residual coordinate vanishes. The joint actual image Xi_q may not be replaced by the product of its projections.

## Scope

This is a rigorous structural theorem, not a computation of delta_p(i,0), the surviving joint images, or the global Tate lift. There is no general non-CM exclusion claim, no assumed sufficiency of scalar height equations, and no unrestricted Bloch-Kato/Mordell-Weil identification. No computational script is represented as proving these cohomological statements.
