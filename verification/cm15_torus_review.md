# Independent audit: the a=15 CM torus obstruction

Verdict: accepted for exclusion of (±i,0) at every good locally visible prime, under the stated Mordell–Weil abelian-image convention.

## Exact algebra and rank

The CM map was independently checked to preserve E, have differential multiplier sqrt(-3), fix T, and square to [-3] in both coordinates by comparison with rational tripling. Its degree is three; conjugation and Rosati signs follow. The discriminant support is precisely {2,3}.

The two-isogeny descent certificate was checked: the classes supplied by T and (−2,8) give the stated subgroup; both complementary d=2 quartics are impossible over Q2. Exhausting primitive pairs modulo8 against squares modulo64 independently reproduces both obstructions. The short equation is z³−60z+176. A draft constant128 was corrected to176 before publication, and the exact expansion was rerun. The coordinate185/4 for 2P on that model proves P nontorsion by Nagell–Lutz.

## Complete divisor and exceptional charts

The X-difference, Y-ratio, residual coordinates and signs were checked symbolically. All six Weierstrass points are simple in both origin divisors; F(±s)=64 ensures no missing multiplicity. The residual points over x=∓s/3 are simple. Checks at zero, infinity and ±1 show no further origins. Thus each divisor has degree8, with their difference exactly div((3x+s)/(3x−s)).

## Descent and origin-fiber ratio

The norm-one descent exchanges the two line factors and inverts the fiber coordinate. The principal divisor makes the curve lift global.

At W, m+=m−=O, so substituting sigma=1 would be wrong. The coefficient in a common local frame is the limit t(f+)/t(f−)=(i−s)/(i+s). Dividing by g(i)=2+sqrt(3) gives −7+4sqrt(3). This exact unit was checked independently.

## Proper reference sign

On E×{O} the trivialization is canonical. On {T}×E the symmetric linearization preserving the canonical section has sign+1 at the common image T, but sign−1 at O. Therefore the proper sections −e1,−e2 start at (P,O,−1), agree at (T,O,−1), and end at q=(T,T,+1). The proof retains this sign and endpoint. Both sections descend and use proper elliptic path torsors, hence give the required good-prime crystalline and outside-{2,3,p} unramified reference class.

## Global central fiber and local nonvanishing

Rank one and nonzero elliptic logarithm identify the necessary global abelian projection. The anti-invariant S-unit group for K=Q(sqrt(-3)), S={2,3}, is zero: ordinary units are finite and both S primes are nonsplit. This proves the global finite-central group vanishes.

The proof separately retains exactness of D_cris for the twisted crystalline Lie extension to put differences in H_f^1. Ordinary H^0=0 supplies freeness but would not suffice for that crystalline assertion.

The actual/reference ratio is the norm-one unit −7+4sqrt(3), not a root of unity. Its local logarithm is nonzero, and restriction–corestriction and freeness give the contradiction. Both split p≡1 mod12 and inert p≡5 mod12 are covered, without an ordinary-height hypothesis.
